Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

coldmail ({{APP_URL}}) helps job seekers find people to contact about a role, writes a personal email to each, and sends the ones they approve from their own Gmail. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you control.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics: name, email address and profile picture, when you sign in with Google.
  • Resumes you upload (PDF), and a structured summary of each that we create to match you with people.
  • Bundles you create: the role you describe, the people found for it (names, job titles, companies, public sources, work email addresses), and the emails written for them, including your edits.
  • A record of emails you send through coldmail (recipient, subject, time), so you never contact the same person twice.
  • Your signature and example emails, if you add them.
  • A Gmail authorization token, if you connect Gmail, stored encrypted.

How we use Google user data

If you connect Gmail, coldmail asks only for permission to send email on your behalf (the gmail.send scope). We use it for one thing: sending the specific emails you review and approve in coldmail, at the moment you click send. coldmail cannot read, search, or delete anything in your mailbox.

coldmail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not use it to train AI models, and no person at coldmail reads it except to fix a problem you ask us to look at, or where required by law.

Who processes your data

  • Anthropic (Claude API) reads your resume summary and the role you describe in order to research people and write emails. It also browses public web pages to find them.
  • Hunter and Apollo receive a person's name and company to look up a work email address.
  • Google sends the emails you approve, from your account.
  • Our hosting and database provider stores the data above.

We share data with these services only to provide coldmail, and with no one else.

About the people coldmail finds

coldmail only uses information that people have made public about their work (for example company pages, articles, talks and public profiles), plus work email addresses from lookup services. If you were contacted through coldmail and want your details removed, email us and we'll delete them from every account that holds them.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Gmail at any time on your account page, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete resumes and bundles whenever you like.
  • Delete your account from the account page. This permanently erases your resumes, bundles, history and tokens.

Security and retention

Data travels over HTTPS. Gmail tokens are encrypted at rest. Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies. We keep your data until you delete it or your account.
